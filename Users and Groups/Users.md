## User Management Security Rules

1. Every employee must have an individual account.
2. Accounts must not be shared.
3. Access must be assigned according to job responsibilities.
4. Administrator accounts must be separated from normal user accounts.
5. Inactive accounts must be reviewed and disabled when appropriate.
6. MFA should be required according to the organisation's security policy.
